Heap target solver

Finds every way it can to fill Ocarina of Time's actor heap so that what you choose lands at the address you choose, simplest first. NTSC 1.2 only.

1Starting point

Dolphin Wii VC MEM1 or raw RDRAM. It stays in your browser.

Heap in the dump

Actors in the scene the solver may remove

2End point

It counts if the thing itself, its code or one of its tables starts at the address.

3What Link can use

What it models
Modeled from the decomp
  • Heap allocation exactly as __osMalloc: first fit from the bottom, 16-byte sizes, 0x30-byte headers, a gap split only when the rest is bigger than a header.
  • Top-down allocation (__osMallocR) for persistent actor code, and one shared 0x24E0 block for absolute code (songs, spells, magic arrows).
  • Freeing as __osFree: a freed block merges with free neighbours.
  • Spawning an actor: its code first if not loaded, then the actor, then what its Init allocates (arrow tables, Farore's Wind table, 3 bugs, 19 blue flames).
  • Removing an actor frees it and its tables; normal code is freed with its last actor.
  • Pot, crate and bush drops as Item_DropCollectible decides them for Link's age, items, health and collected flags.
  • At most 3 bombs, bombchus and explosions out at once; one drawn or held item at a time. The thing that lands counts too.
Not modeled
  • Timing. Things that go away on their own (bombs, nuts, the boomerang, drops) stay until a step says they go.
  • Anything else in the scene allocating or freeing meanwhile (enemies, timers, room changes).
  • Enemy drops and death effects when one is killed.
  • Sizes are the simulator's NTSC 1.2 export; other versions differ.
